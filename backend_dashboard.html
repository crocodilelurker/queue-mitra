🎯 Atomic Ticket-Matching Engine

Guarantees that multiple doctors clicking "Call Next" simultaneously can never receive the same patient. Uses MongoDB findOneAndUpdate with sequence sequencing.

Estimated Wait = round5( ceil(peopleAhead / doctorsOnDuty) × avgConsultTime + delay )
🚨 Emergency Safety & Audit SLA

Strict adherence to the hackathon rule: The app never triages emergencies as safe. Red-flag symptoms trigger immediate directions to Casualty and 112/108 numbers.

"I Need Urgent Help" -> Sound Chime to Staff -> Logs responseTimeSeconds SLA
